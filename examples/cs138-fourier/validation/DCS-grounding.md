## Notation and vocabulary decisions

- **Eigenfunctions:** the new deck uses `φₙ` for complex periodic modes and `yₙ` for the familiar real/generalized modes. L7's arbitrary amplitude inside an eigenfunction is set to one when choosing a basis; the expansion coefficient then records its amount. This does not change the eigenspace.
- **Coefficients:** uppercase `Cₙ` denotes complex Fourier coefficients; `Dₙ` denotes solution coefficients; lowercase `aₙ,bₙ` denotes the DCS real coefficients; lowercase `cₙ` denotes generalized expansion coefficients. In particular, `C₀=a₀`, whereas Chen writes `c₀=a₀/2` under his different constant convention.
- **Language:** “eigenvalue BVP,” “orthogonal functions,” “inner product,” “Sturm–Liouville,” and “generalized Fourier series” follow the course material. “Mode,” “frequency,” “coordinates,” and “freeze” are explanatory vocabulary tied explicitly to those objects, not new prerequisites.
