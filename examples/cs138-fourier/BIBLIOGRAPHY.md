# Full bibliography

Source IDs are used in the lecturer notes. Slide footers show their short labels. Online titles below are clickable. The canonical metadata is in `references.typ`.

## L7 — CS138 L7

**Justin Jose Ruaya and John Justine Villar.** Boundary Value Problems: Eigenvalue Problems, Sturm-Liouville Theory, Power Series.

CS 138, S1 AY 2026–2027. Supplied CS138_L7_BVP1.zip; L7_EigenvalueBVP_Sturm.tex, especially the periodic eigenvalue BVP.

**Use in this lecture:** Course prerequisite: boundary conditions select eigenfunctions. L denotes the full period in this source.

## V21 — Villar 2021

**John Justine S. Villar.** Fourier Series and Fourier Series Representation of Functions.

CS 138, 1S 2021–2022, University of the Philippines Diliman. Supplied CS 138 Fourier Series.pdf; PDF pp. 18–38.

**Use in this lecture:** Course notation and real Fourier examples: period 2L, constant A₀ without a factor 1/2.

## H24 — Hernandez 2024

**Nestine Hope S. Hernandez.** Fourier Series.

CS 138, University of the Philippines Diliman, ©2024. Supplied CS138_Lec3.pdf; PDF pp. 9–10, 28–44.

**Use in this lecture:** Course convention a₀ = mean; orthogonal projection and generalized Sturm–Liouville expansion.

## C1 — Chen ch. 1

**W. W. L. Chen.** Introduction to Fourier Series.

Fourier Series and Integral Transforms, chapter 1, pp. 1–6, ©2004, 2013. Supplied fst01.pdf.

**Use in this lecture:** Partial sums, periodic extension, endpoint behavior, and the distinction between formal expansion and convergence.

## C3 — Chen §3.2

**W. W. L. Chen.** Fourier Coefficients.

Fourier Series and Integral Transforms, chapter 3, pp. 19–27, ©2004, 2013. Supplied fst03.pdf; §3.2, equations (3.6)–(3.9), and Exercise 7(i).

**Use in this lecture:** Complex Fourier coefficients, positive-exponent synthesis, negative-exponent analysis, conjugate symmetry, and real/complex conversion. Uses a₀/2.

## H43 — Herman §4.3

**Russell Herman.** [4.3: Connections to Linear Algebra](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Differential_Equations/A_Second_Course_in_Ordinary_Differential_Equations%3A_Dynamical_Systems_and_Boundary_Value_Problems_%28Herman%29/04%3A_Boundary_Value_Problems/4.03%3A_Connections_to_Linear_Algebra).

A Second Course in Ordinary Differential Equations: Dynamical Systems and Boundary Value Problems. Especially the generalized basis expansion. Mathematics LibreTexts.

**Use in this lecture:** Finite vector coordinates motivate function inner products and orthogonal expansion coefficients.

## H62 — Herman §6.2

**Russell Herman.** [6.2: Properties of Sturm-Liouville Eigenvalue Problems](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Differential_Equations/A_Second_Course_in_Ordinary_Differential_Equations%3A_Dynamical_Systems_and_Boundary_Value_Problems_%28Herman%29/06%3A_Sturm_Liouville/6.02%3A_Properties_of_Sturm-Liouville_Eigenvalue_Problems).

A Second Course in Ordinary Differential Equations: Dynamical Systems and Boundary Value Problems. Regular Sturm–Liouville properties and completeness. Mathematics LibreTexts.

**Use in this lecture:** Weighted orthogonality, completeness, and generalized coefficients; apply each theorem with its boundary-condition hypotheses.

## T111 — Trench §11.1

**William F. Trench.** [11.1: Eigenvalue Problems for y″ + λy = 0](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Differential_Equations/Elementary_Differential_Equations_with_Boundary_Value_Problems_%28Trench%29/11%3A_Boundary_Value_Problems_and_Fourier_Expansions/11.01%3A_Eigenvalue_Problems_for_y%27%27__y__0).

Elementary Differential Equations with Boundary Value Problems. Problem 5 is the periodic problem on [−L,L]. Mathematics LibreTexts.

**Use in this lecture:** Boundary-selected frequencies, the constant mode, paired sine/cosine eigenfunctions, and trigonometric orthogonality.

## T112 — Trench §11.2

**William F. Trench.** [11.2: Fourier Series I](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Differential_Equations/Elementary_Differential_Equations_with_Boundary_Value_Problems_%28Trench%29/11%3A_Boundary_Value_Problems_and_Fourier_Expansions/11.02%3A_Fourier_Series_I).

Elementary Differential Equations with Boundary Value Problems. Fourier coefficients; Definition 11.2.3 and Theorem 11.2.4. Mathematics LibreTexts.

**Use in this lecture:** Full real series with a₀ = mean, and piecewise-smooth convergence including periodic endpoint limits.

## T113 — Trench §11.3

**William F. Trench.** [11.3: Fourier Series II](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Differential_Equations/Elementary_Differential_Equations_with_Boundary_Value_Problems_%28Trench%29/11%3A_Boundary_Value_Problems_and_Fourier_Expansions/11.03%3A_Fourier_Series_II).

Elementary Differential Equations with Boundary Value Problems. Fourier cosine, sine, and mixed expansions. Mathematics LibreTexts.

**Use in this lecture:** Optional follow-up: half-range extensions and how changing endpoint conditions changes the available modes.

## T132 — Trench §13.2

**William F. Trench.** [13.2: Sturm-Liouville Problems](https://math.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Differential_Equations/Elementary_Differential_Equations_with_Boundary_Value_Problems_%28Trench%29/13%3A_Boundary_Value_Problems_for_Second_Order_Linear_Equations/13.02%3A_Sturm-Liouville_Problems).

Elementary Differential Equations with Boundary Value Problems. Theorems 13.2.4 and 13.2.7; Examples 13.2.1–13.2.4. Mathematics LibreTexts.

**Use in this lecture:** Weighted orthogonality and generalized eigenfunction expansion; separated-boundary results must not be used to assert periodic simplicity.

## S19 — Sanderson 2019

**Grant Sanderson; text adaptation by Vivek Verma.** [But what is a Fourier series? From heat flow to circle drawings](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/fourier-series/).

3Blue1Brown, 30 June 2019. Sections: Waves and rotation; The integration trick; Pull out any other term.

**Use in this lecture:** Visual motivation: rotations, averaging the constant term, then counter-rotation to isolate another coefficient. The average is uniform in the input parameter.

## O24 — Orloff §4.6

**Jeremy Orloff.** [Complex Numbers and Exponentials](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/es-1803-differential-equations-spring-2024/mites_1803_s24_topic4.pdf).

MIT ES.1803 Differential Equations, Spring 2024, Topic 4 notes, §§4.6–4.6.2, pp. 5–6. MIT OpenCourseWare.

**Use in this lecture:** Euler's formula, consistency with the power series, differentiation with respect to a real variable, and the polar interpretation.

## FT09 — MIT 6.003 L17

**MIT 6.003; lecturer Dennis M. Freeman.** [Fourier Transform](https://web.mit.edu/6.003/F09/www/handouts/lec17-handout-6up.pdf).

Signals and Systems, Lecture 17, 10 November 2009. Six-slides-per-page handout, especially PDF pp. 1–2.

**Use in this lecture:** Optional bridge: increasing the period decreases frequency spacing; scaled Fourier coefficients lead to the transform and synthesis integral.

## LP08 — Rowell 2008

**D. Rowell.** [The Laplace Transform](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-161-signal-processing-continuous-and-discrete-fall-2008/6e6f94d1e27d4fe8249d3123a3c85e0c_laplace.pdf).

MIT 2.161 Signal Processing: Continuous and Discrete, 23 September 2008. Equations (6), (7), and (11), PDF pp. 3–4. MIT OpenCourseWare.

**Use in this lecture:** Optional one-sided connection: e^(−st) = e^(−σt)e^(−iωt), with s = σ + iω, and the region-of-convergence qualification.

## H1822 — Fourier 1822

**Jean Baptiste Joseph Fourier.** [Théorie Analytique de la Chaleur: Introduction](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/theorie-analytique-de-la-chaleur/introduction/48689FD8C6E4E1EC960AE49F27E47450).

Original French work, first published 1822; chapter I, pp. 1–98, especially article 1. Cambridge Library Collection reissue, 2009; online 2010. DOI: 10.1017/CBO9780511693229.002.

**Use in this lecture:** Historical primary text: Fourier frames heat propagation as determining later temperatures from an initial distribution. The Cambridge record confirms the original 1822 publication date.

## HIST37 — Sturm–Liouville 1837

**C. Sturm and J. Liouville.** [Extrait d'un Mémoire sur le développement des fonctions en séries dont les différents termes sont assujettis à satisfaire à une même équation différentielle linéaire, contenant un paramètre variable](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1837_1_2__220_0/).

Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées, series 1, volume 2 (1837), pp. 220–223. Original article digitized by Gallica and catalogued by Numdam.

**Use in this lecture:** Historical primary source for generalized series built from solutions of a parameter-dependent differential equation. Supports the 1837 date without asserting a modern theorem in its full present-day form.

## F14 — Freeman et al. 2014

**Scott Freeman, Sarah L. Eddy, Miles McDonough, Michelle K. Smith, Nnadozie Okoroafor, Hannah Jordt, and Mary Pat Wenderoth.** [Active learning increases student performance in science, engineering, and mathematics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4060654/).

PNAS 111(23), 8410–8415 (2014). DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1319030111.

**Use in this lecture:** Instructor preparation: broad evidence for active participation in undergraduate STEM; does not validate a unique Fourier lecture sequence.

## C04 — Crouch et al. 2004

**Catherine H. Crouch, A. P. Fagen, J. P. Callan, and E. Mazur.** [Classroom demonstrations: Learning tools or entertainment?](https://works.swarthmore.edu/fac-physics/203/).

American Journal of Physics 72(6), 835–838 (2004). DOI: 10.1119/1.1707018. Author's institutional repository record and abstract.

**Use in this lecture:** Instructor preparation: ask learners to commit to a prediction before a demonstration; evidence comes from physics demonstrations.

## SB98 — Schwartz–Bransford 1998

**Daniel L. Schwartz and John D. Bransford.** [A Time for Telling](https://aaalab.stanford.edu/papers/time_for_telling.pdf).

Cognition and Instruction 16(4), 475–522 (1998). Author-hosted copy.

**Use in this lecture:** Instructor preparation: contrasting cases can prepare students to learn from an explanation; the original studies used psychology materials.
